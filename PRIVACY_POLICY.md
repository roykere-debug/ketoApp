# Privacy Policy

**Last Updated:** February 19, 2026

## 1. Introduction

MyKeto ("we," "us," "our," or "Company") operates the MyKeto mobile application (the "App"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our App.

**Please read this Privacy Policy carefully.** If you do not agree with our policies and practices, please do not use our App.

## 2. Information We Collect

### 2.1 Information You Provide Directly

**Account Information:**
- Email address
- Password (encrypted)
- Name, age, gender, height, weight

**Health & Nutrition Data:**
- Meal logs (food items, quantities, nutritional values)
- Daily macronutrient goals (calories, protein, fat, carbohydrates)
- Activity level
- Keto score assessments

**Camera/Photo Data:**
- Photos of food taken through the scanner feature (for AI analysis only; not stored permanently unless you save the meal)

**Communications:**
- Chat messages with the AI health coach
- Support inquiries or feedback

### 2.2 Information Collected Automatically

**Device Information:**
- Device type, operating system, unique identifiers
- App version and settings

**Usage Analytics:**
- Features used, frequency of use
- Timestamps of app activity
- Error logs and crash reports

**Location:**
- General location data (country/region) inferred from IP address for analytics only

## 3. How We Use Your Information

We use collected information for:

1. **Core Functionality**
   - Creating and managing your account
   - Logging meals and tracking nutrition
   - Calculating daily macronutrient goals
   - Providing AI-powered food scanning and recipe suggestions
   - Operating the AI health coach chatbot

2. **Personalization**
   - Customizing your experience based on profile data
   - Generating personalized nutrition recommendations
   - Improving the accuracy of food analysis

3. **Service Improvement**
   - Analyzing app usage to improve features
   - Identifying and fixing technical issues
   - Understanding user preferences

4. **Communication**
   - Sending important account or service notifications
   - Responding to support requests
   - Sending newsletters (only if you opt-in)

5. **Legal & Safety**
   - Complying with legal obligations
   - Detecting and preventing fraud or abuse
   - Protecting our rights and your safety

## 4. Data Retention

**Account Data:** Retained while your account is active. Upon deletion, your account and associated data are removed within 30 days.

**Meal Logs:** Stored as long as your account is active for historical tracking.

**Photos:** Temporary food photos used for scanning are deleted immediately after analysis unless saved as part of a meal log.

**Chat History:** Stored for 90 days, then automatically deleted unless you request permanent storage.

**Analytics Data:** Aggregated and anonymized data retained for up to 1 year.

## 5. Data Sharing & Third-Party Services

### 5.1 Third-Party Service Providers

We use the following third-party services that may access your data:

**Google Gemini API**
- Purpose: AI food scanning, recipe generation, health coaching
- Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
- Data shared: Photos (for scanning), nutritional queries

**Supabase (Backend Infrastructure)**
- Purpose: Database storage, authentication, API hosting
- Privacy Policy: https://supabase.com/privacy
- Data shared: All account and meal data

**Expo (App Hosting & Updates)**
- Purpose: App distribution and over-the-air updates
- Privacy Policy: https://expo.dev/privacy
- Data shared: Device information, app version, usage analytics

### 5.2 Data Sharing Limitations

**We DO NOT:**
- Sell or rent your personal data to third parties
- Share meal logs or health data with advertisers
- Share data without your explicit consent (except as required by law)
- Use your data for marketing purposes without opt-in

**We MAY disclose your data if:**
- Required by law (court order, subpoena)
- Necessary to protect our rights, privacy, safety, or property
- In connection with a sale or merger of our company

## 6. Data Security

**Security Measures:**
- All data transmitted to our servers uses HTTPS encryption
- Passwords are hashed and salted
- Sensitive data (API keys) stored securely with environment variables
- Regular security audits and updates

**Limitations:**
- No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure
- We cannot guarantee absolute security of your data
- You are responsible for keeping your password confidential

## 7. Your Privacy Rights

### 7.1 Access & Portability
- You can request a copy of your personal data at any time
- We will provide it in a structured, portable format within 30 days

### 7.2 Correction & Deletion
- You can update your profile information in the app
- You can request deletion of your account and all associated data
- Account deletion is permanent; data cannot be recovered

### 7.3 Opt-Out Options
- Disable analytics in app settings

### 7.3 Contact for Privacy Requests

## 8. Children's Privacy

MyKeto is not intended for children under 13 years old. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we become aware that we have collected data from a child under 13, we will delete it immediately.

For users aged 13-18, parental consent is recommended.

## 9. International Data Transfers

Your data may be transferred to and stored in countries outside your country of residence. These countries may have different data protection laws than your home country. By using MyKeto, you consent to this transfer.

**Specifically:**
- Our servers are hosted on Supabase infrastructure (primarily in US/EU regions)
- Google Gemini API processes data globally

You can disable analytics in app settings at any time.
